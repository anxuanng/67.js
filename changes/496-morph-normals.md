- Light morphs that have no authored base NORMAL (#496): the loader now computes the base normals from the triangles and per-target normal deltas from the morphed positions, so lighting follows the morph instead of dropping the deltas. A NaN or Infinity in a vertex POSITION or a morph POSITION delta becomes 0, and a non-finite inverse bind matrix becomes the identity, each with a load warning, so bounds, culling and skinning stay finite instead of refusing the file.
