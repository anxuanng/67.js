- entry:core: the renderer adds a map's `bounce` colour to the first irradiance coefficient (one small method), which is what lets a lamp-lit room get its bounced light with no shader change and no cost per frame.
- entry:ground-bounce: the closed-room bounce estimate (exposed wall area, mean albedo, lamp flux) lives in the ground-bounce add-on, where the floor's bounce already is; it lifts lamp-lit rooms from about half to about the brightness of Cycles.
- scene:lit-box: carries the renderer method above.
- scene:gltf-box: carries the renderer method above.
- scene:gltf-box+lazy: carries the renderer method above.
- scene:agent-scene: carries the renderer method above and the second `scene_inspect` message for a closed room lit by lamps only, which names the bake preset.
- scene:agent-scene+lazy: same as scene:agent-scene.
- entry:ground-bounce: the low-sun fill (a constant bounce colour from the sunlit slopes and walls around a shadow) lives in the ground-bounce add-on, next to the floor and room bounce; core and lit-box do not grow.
