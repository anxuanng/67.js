- Match the pure-leaf luminance of lower tree levels to level 0 (issue #432). The level match in `src/tree-sky.js` now takes the mean sky visibility over the leaf each vertex stands for (`vertexCoverage`, a third of the area of its triangles) instead of over the vertices, because a clump has few vertices for a lot of leaf. Each species also carries a colour gain and a sky gain per lower level in `src/tree-level-gains.js`, solved by `node scripts/measure-tree-level-luminance.mjs --fit`, which draws each level against level 0 from three sides under sky light, a sun behind and a sun in front, and bring all three ratios close to 1; the file holds the geometric mean of fits on seeds 1 to 6, and seeds 7 and 9 (held out) get closer to level 0 than before for every species and level. The gains are capped at colour 1.6 and sky 2 because larger ones made the mountain-valley reference scene score worse against Cycles. `tests/tree-sky-levels.test.js` pins the coverage-weighted mean and the range of the gains; its old pin on the plain vertex mean changes because the method changes. This changes how far trees look.
