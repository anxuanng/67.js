- Fix the opt-in `bake.irradianceGrid` lifting closed rooms (issue #506). Against Cycles on the five reference halls the grid scored 0.15 to 0.19 FLIP and was 1.6 to 1.8 times too bright; it now scores 0.0576, 0.0569, 0.1455, 0.1211 and 0.1077 (temple-hall, dark-shaft, temple-hall-a, -b, -c), better than the lightmap bake on temple-hall-a and -b and within 0.003 on temple-hall and dark-shaft, in about a second where the bake takes 14 to 57. Three causes: the world's light reached the grid's surfaces unoccluded (each node now stores the share of sky it sees and the shader scales the environment light by it, as the lightmap path does with its sky visibility map); nodes inside a wall were filled with the mean of both sides, which carried the sunlit outside into the room (they now copy the darkest neighbour); and the smoothing blurred across walls (a neighbour counts only when a ray between the two nodes is free). A grid scene also no longer gets the sun-bounce point lights or the ground bounce, which doubled the light the grid already holds. `bake.irradianceGrid` stays opt-in. The reference harness takes `--bake-grid [nodes]`.
