- Fix `environment.sunBounce` doing nothing in a sunlit room with a window opening and a garden outside it (issue #462). The room test started its rays from the middle of the scene's bounds, which a lawn and a hedge beside the window moved out of the room (share 0.50 against the 0.90 needed), so no patch lights were found and `sunBounce` 1 and 2 drew the same picture. `fittedClosedRoomShare` (`src/fitted-closed-room.js`, lazy) starts again from the middle of the parts near the rest and leaves out the heaviest parts when the scene is over 150,000 triangles, so scanned props no longer switch the test off; the core `closedRoomShare` and the inspect diagnostic are unchanged, and open scenes still do not count as rooms. The patch search now samples only the triangles a coarse first pass found sunlit and inside the room (a lawn took nearly all 20,000 samples before, leaving 149 in the room), and a patch's light stands at most half the free distance in front of it, so it cannot end up behind a wall. On the five reference halls `sunBounce` 1 moves the FLIP score against Cycles by -0.0027 to +0.0018 compared with no bounce (the first version: -0.0043 to +0.0022) and the picture around the lit patch toward Cycles; `sunBounce: 0` draws the same pixels as before the bounce existed and open scenes do not change. The default is now 3 (it was 1; the value multiplies the fitted gain, 0 is off), a stronger look for a sun-lit room that sets no `environment.sunBounce`; a patch with less than 3 percent of the scene size of free space in front of it is left out, so no light stands close enough to a surface to draw a hard speck. Numbers in `docs/lighting.md`.
