- Add `volumeFog.shadowAmbient` (0 to 1, default 0.15, the value that was hard coded in `src/post.js`): the share of the fog's `ambient` that stays where the sun's shadow covers it. A closed room sees little sky, so 0.15 holds and no existing recipe changes. Open ground under a sky sees all of it, so `fog-valley` sets 1 and goes from 0.1967 to 0.1855 against Cycles, past EEVEE's 0.1952 (part of issue #165). Measured on the glow weight and the shadow floor first: the glow weight lowered `fog-valley` only at the cost of `fog` (+0.02) and `temple-hall` (+0.008), and a global floor of 0.4 cost `fog-room` +0.04, so neither could be one constant for rooms and open ground.
