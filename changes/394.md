Part of #394.

The mist now has the sky's mean radiance (0.5), and the forest pixels read 0.030 to 0.037 luminance against 0.014 to 0.021 before and 0.020 to 0.033 in Cycles (FLIP against Cycles 0.200 to 0.167).
