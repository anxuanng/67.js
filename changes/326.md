- Cloth follows moving characters (issue #326). Colliders can be a ball or capsule attached to any node or bone, or one capsule per bone of an armature fitted as the ragdoll does; a sheet's `pinTo` makes its pinned edges ride a node with a pin weight. Both are sampled at every 60 Hz solver step, so a seek equals playing to that time. A cape pinned to the reference walker stays outside every limb capsule through a 5 s walk, and the same cape without colliders sinks 20 mm or more into them. Up to 64 moving colliders; a 5 s walk bakes in about 2.5 s.
