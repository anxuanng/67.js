- `createTerrain({ mirrorLevels })` (default 0) builds up to that many extra coarser meshes for each chunk, used only by the water mirror draw. The renderer never picks a `mirrorOnly` level for the main camera, so main-camera pixels do not change; the mirror picks them by the screen error of the mirror's own camera. Terrain meshes of a chunk are not edge-matched across mirror levels, so a seam can show in the blurred reflection. Part of #316.
- `lods` entries accept `mirrorOnly: true`: the level is skipped in a normal frame and in the shadow draw, and chosen by the water mirror draw like any other level.
