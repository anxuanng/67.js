- `createTerrain({ mirrorLevels })` (default 0) builds up to that many extra coarser meshes for each chunk, used only by the water mirror draw (part of #316). Their chunk edges keep every finest-grid vertex, so they meet a neighbour's finest edge or another mirror mesh with no crack (a plain coarse mesh left gaps of up to 3 m on a hilly test terrain). `attachWaterReflection` hides these levels from every other draw and lets the mirror pick them by its own screen error, so a terrain that sets `mirrorLevels` must be used with it. No renderer code changed.
