- Make reversed-Z depth camera-relative (issue #310). `installReversedDepth` now compiles the main vertex shader with the eye subtracted from the world position before the view-projection multiply, so large world coordinates no longer meet the view translation in float32; the renderer core is unchanged and a scene without the addon draws byte for byte as before. On the depth-precision bench (GPU) the 500 m, 0.1 mm gap pair falls from 0.134 to 0.069 wrong pixels and the 500 m, 1 mm pair from 0.015 to 0.014; the rest of the 0.069 is the float32 step of the model's own vertices at that distance, which no vertex transform removes.
