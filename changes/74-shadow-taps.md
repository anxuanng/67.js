- Make the sun shadow filter one hardware comparison fetch per tap instead of four texel fetches (part of issue #74). The shadow depth texture now compares on fetch with linear filtering (`TEXTURE_COMPARE_MODE`), so the 9-tap filter costs 9 fetches, not 36, with the same result (every reference scene is within 0.0001 of Cycles before and after). On SwiftShader, alternated with three.js, the frame fell from 8.4 to 7.1 times three.js on `transparency`, 7.6 to 6.6 on `skinned-characters`, 5.5 to 4.0 on `instanced-objects` and 3.8 to 3.5 on `moving-shadows`. The fog volume reads the shadow through the same sampler, so its single tap is now filtered over 2 by 2 texels. Cutting the filter to 4 taps saved only 4 percent more and was not kept.
