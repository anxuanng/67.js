- The fog's ambient in the sun's shadow now follows a per-point sky visibility when `Renderer67` gets the new `fogSkyVisibility` option from `src/fog-sky-visibility.js` (the agent preview and the reference harness pass it). It casts 24 upward rays from the centre of each cell of a grid of at most 2,048 cells over the fog box, once per scene and fog box, and uploads one 8-bit 3D texture; fog that sees any sky keeps all of the ambient and fog that sees none keeps 0.1 of it. Without the option, without `volumeFog`, or when a recipe sets `shadowAmbient`, the post shader has the sampling compiled out and draws as before. Against Cycles on the GPU: `dark-shaft` 0.0607 to 0.0559 (region 0.1802 to 0.1282), `temple-hall` 0.0674 to 0.0640 (region 0.2723 to 0.2611; baked region 0.2439 to 0.2335), `fog-room` 0.1049 to 0.1036 (region 0.2405 to 0.1625); every other scene is unchanged to four digits. The `temple-hall` region without a bake does not yet beat EEVEE (0.2455). The core entry grows 141 bytes gzip (part of issue #164).
