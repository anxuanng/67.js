- Allow `bake.bounces` up to 8 (was 3) for the lightmap bake and the irradiance grid (issue #462). Cycles gathers 8 bounces and the grid stopped at 3, which left sunlit rooms cooler and darker: on a synthetic room with an orange floor and a warm sun, 8 bounces read 28% warmer (R/B) and 30% brighter than 3, and the grid matched its own 3-bounce gather within 2%. On the agent-built reading-corner room with the grid, 8 bounces move R/B from 1.22 to 1.27 (Cycles 1.44) and FLIP against Cycles from 0.4035 to 0.3817. The default stays 3, so no scene changes unless a recipe asks for more. The reference harness gets `--bake-bounces <n>`.
