- Measure the impostor forest on terrain and bake it faster (part of #324). `scripts/measure-vegetation-forest.mjs` now draws the 5,000-tree forest on rolling `createTerrain` ground and prints triangles and draw calls counted at the WebGL calls, GPU frame time per interleaved run and the bake time per repeat (`--gpu`, `--runs N`). Impostors bake from level 3, the mesh the card replaces, which cut the FLIP of the card render against the full-level render on the RTX 3060 from 0.0080 to 0.0039. The bake loop steps its barycentric weights along a row and reuses its bleed buffers, so baking 15 tree variants took 1.53 to 1.55 s on the RTX 3060 machine in headless Chrome. Impostors stay opt-in: no default look changes. See https://67js.codepawl.com/vegetation.md.
