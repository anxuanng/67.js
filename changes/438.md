- Water shadows on the bed follow the sun's refracted path (issue #438). The sun leg to a submerged surface is absorbed over `depth / cos(theta_t)` with Snell n = 1.33, so the clear lake at 4 m keeps 0.06 of its shadow contrast in red, as in Cycles, not 0.23. In murky water the lift that pulled the shadow toward the lit side is replaced by a positive in-scatter term, so a shadow never inverts and a river at 4 m keeps about a quarter of its contrast. Contrast for the lake and river at 0.2, 1 and 4 m is within 0.05 of the Cycles reference.
