- Stop z-fighting on flush surfaces (part of #310): `renderer.diagnostics` and `scene_inspect` warn `coplanar-overlap` with both part names, the shared area and a fix, and a node's `decal: true` or `depthLayer` (0 to 4), or `environment.resolveCoplanar: 'offset'`, draws the smaller part with a scoped polygon offset in the colour pass and render passes without moving geometry.
- Add reversed-Z depth as an addon (part of #310): `installReversedDepth(renderer, mode)` in `src/reversed-depth.js` draws the colour pass with depth 1 at the near plane, a 32-bit float depth buffer and `GREATER` where `EXT_clip_control` exists, and leaves the shadow maps, backgrounds and post passes on the standard range. `mode` is `true`, `false` or `'auto'`, which switches it on when a frame's far / near passes 10,000 or the coplanar check (`installDepthPrecision`, which also installs the warning) finds a flush pair more than 40 units from the eye. The renderer core is unchanged, so a scene that does not install it pays no bytes (lit-box +0). On the RTX 3060 the share of pixels where the far plane of a pair wins falls from 0.15 to 0.009 (50 m, 0.1 mm gap), 0.079 to 0 (50 m, 1 mm) and 0.188 to 0.015 (500 m, 1 mm); 500 m with 0.1 mm stays at 0.13 (`bench/reference/README.md`).
