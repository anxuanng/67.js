- Give smooth metal parts a reflection probe by default (part of issue #167, which stays open). A recipe part with `metallic` 0.5 or more and `roughness` 0.6 or less (up to 8 such parts) now gets a probe traced from the scene around it, so a chrome sphere shows the floor and its neighbours; `environment.objectReflections: false` turns it off and `scene_inspect` reports `reflections`. Against Cycles `metal` goes from 0.1294 to 0.1036 (EEVEE 0.0916), `brushed-film` 0.1149 to 0.1029, `car-paint` 0.1059 to 0.1038, `material-spheres` 0.1312 to 0.1293 and `metal-room` 0.5128 to 0.5076. A scene with no reflective part draws the same parts and keeps its frame time; `metal` costs about 0.03 ms more on the GPU.
