- The path tracer now honours light linking (part of issue #215). A light with `affects`, and the sun with `environment.keyAffects`, adds nothing at a part it skips, as direct light and at every bounce, and still casts shadows for the others; it reads the same per-part `material.lightMask` as the renderer and the light bake. `report.ignored` no longer lists linking. The `light-linking` scene against Cycles goes from 0.6518 to 0.0213 (the rasterizer scores 0.0998); scenes without links draw the same pixels as before, and the reference harness now passes its path-traced scenes through `applyLightLinks`.
