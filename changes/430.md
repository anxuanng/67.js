- Cloth steps live: `createCloth(positions, triangles, options)` returns a stepper with `step(dt)`, runtime `pin`/`unpin` with moving targets, `pinForce`, per-particle extra mass and tearing (`tearStretch`, `tearForce`, a readable `broken` list). `bakeCloth` is now a loop over it and records the same frames as before (closes #430).
- New addon `@codepawlhq/67js/addons/update-part-vertices` exports `updatePartVertices(renderer, part, positions, normals)`, which rewrites a rigid part's vertex buffers with `bufferSubData` instead of `replaceParts`. The core bundle does not change.
- A cloth seek in the MCP preview rewrites one geometry per sheet in place and uploads its positions and normals with `updatePartVertices`, instead of replacing the part (part of #430). One 33 x 33 seek and draw measured 1.53 ms with `replaceParts` and 1.07 ms in place.
