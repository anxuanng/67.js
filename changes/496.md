- `loadGltf` and `parseGltf` no longer refuse a file over three more Sketchfab-style defects (issue #496): authored vertex normals of zero length are rebuilt from the triangles, a mesh whose node has a zero or non-finite world transform is skipped, and morph targets without a base `NORMAL` load with generated normals and no morph normal deltas. Each repair adds an entry to `asset.warnings` that names the mesh, primitive or node.
