- Fit the default fog to the scene size (issue #263). A scene that sets no `fogDensity` now gets 0.006, or 1.2 divided by the scene diagonal when that is smaller, so a 900 m scene keeps 30 percent of its contrast at the far side instead of 0.4 percent. Scenes up to about 200 m are unchanged. `normalizeAgentLook` now returns `fogDensity: null` for "fit to the scene"; set a number, or 0 for none, to override.
