- Add a bounced-light estimate for a closed room lit by point, spot or area lights without a bake (issue #351). `groundBounceEnvironment` gives the environment map a `bounce` colour, flux * rho / (pi * area * (1 - rho)) per colour channel, which the renderer adds to the diffuse light at no cost per frame; `environment.roomBounce: false` turns it off. A room with a sun is left alone. `scene_inspect` says so when a closed room is lit by lamps only and names the bake preset.
- Under a low sun (sun elevation near the horizon) the environment map also gets a `bounce` colour from the sunlit slopes and walls around a shadow: `0.4 * albedo * sun / pi * (wall - ground)^2`, free per frame. `mountain-valley` and `megacity-dusk` shadow p20 against Cycles go from 0.60 and 0.59 to 0.79 and 0.85 with a better FLIP; scenes with a high sun, `temple-hall` and `dark-shaft` do not change. `environment.outdoorFill: false` turns it off. `bench/reference/render-gpu.mjs` takes `--cpu` for SwiftShader.
