- Shrink the bush's far crown clumps (issue #394). A bush's crown is wide and its twigs are short, so the clumps that stand in for its leaves at levels 2 and 3 were sized from the widest leaf and reached 1.7 times further out than level 0 (leaf radius median 3.4 m against 2.0 m, from 0.2 m below the ground to 8.5 m on a 6 m bush). They covered 4.5 times the pixels of level 0 (96,000 against 21,000) and sat in each other's shade, so the pure-leaf luminance fell to 0.47 of level 0 under sky light and with the sun behind. The bush species now sets `clumpScale: 0.35`, which keeps the clumps within the level-0 crown; level 2 reads 0.81 of level 0 under sky light, 0.86 with the sun behind and 1.35 with the sun in front, level 3 0.63, 0.70 and 1.17. The other species and the reference frames (`foliage`, `mountain-valley`, `mountain-valley-dawn`: FLIP 0.0, no changed pixel) do not move.
