- Water shadows follow the water (issue #349). A sun shadow on a `water` surface is faint and never darkens the reflection, and everything below the water level fades by `exp(-absorption * depth)` per colour, with shadows lifted and softened by depth times `turbidity`. Recipes take the `water` material tag with `waterPreset` (`lake`, `sea`, `river`), `absorption` (RGB per metre) and `turbidity` (0 to 1), listed in `scene_capabilities` under `water`. The code sits in the world material addon, so a renderer without water does not carry it, and the MCP tool count is unchanged.
