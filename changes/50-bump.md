- Bake the normal map of a graph material at twice the colour resolution, up to 1024 texels (issue #50). A joint edge a few texels wide lost tilt to the bilinear blend of unit normals, 3.5 degrees on average against Blender's Bump node; at 1024 texels it is 2.1. `brick-graph` goes from 0.0734 to 0.0714 against Cycles (FLIP, RTX 3060); a graph whose height is too costly to read four times per texel at that size keeps the colour resolution. The map holds 4 times the texels (5.6 MB with mips for a 512 pattern, 1.4 MB before) and the bake takes about 4 times longer (0.35 s for the reference brick wall).
