- A large flat smooth surface (a lake, a river, a polished floor) now reflects the scene: the new addon `67js/addons/water-reflection` draws the scene from the camera's mirror image into a half-size target, with Fresnel, ripples and a slight blur. `mountain-valley-a` goes from 0.3530 to 0.2562 against Cycles and `megacity-dusk-b` from 0.1575 to 0.1377. Scenes without such a surface are unchanged (issue #195).
