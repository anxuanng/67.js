- Fog near a glowing window or a lamp now picks up that light (part of issue #164). `fogSkyVisibility` also builds a grid of the light that emissive parts and point lights give their surroundings (`fogBounceGrid` in `src/fog-bounce.js`, one bounce, 64 rays per cell, built once per scene and fog box) and the post shader adds it to the fog's scattering. The `temple-hall` fog region against Cycles goes from 0.2611 to 0.2421, past EEVEE (0.2455), and the baked one from 0.2335 to 0.2236; scenes without emissive parts or point lights, and scenes without the option, draw as before. The sun's bounce is left out because it made `dark-shaft` worse.
