- Add baked impostor levels and dithered cross-fades to the vegetation addon (issue #324). `selectLevels(instances, eye, { impostors: { from: 3 }, fade: 0.06 })` draws far trees as one camera-facing card from a 64-view hemi-octahedral colour and normal atlas baked on the CPU (0.6 to 0.7 s for five species in Node), lit by the scene and casting a shadow from the light's side, and cross-fades neighbouring levels with a dither coverage, chosen from the distance alone. A 5,223-tree forest drops from 2.68M to 0.52M triangles with cards from level 3 and 0.36M from level 2 (measured in Node). Both are opt-in, so existing scenes draw as before.
- Impostor cards blend the four baked views around the viewing direction (a stochastic per-pixel pick, so a circling camera no longer steps between 64 views), and `selectLevels` takes `pixelError: { pixels, height, fov }` to set the switch distances from a screen error (part of #324). A frame-difference test checks that a dolly across the card boundary shows no pop.
