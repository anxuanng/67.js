- Irradiance grid (issue #506): `irradianceGrid: true` now picks up to 48 nodes along the longest side within 8,192 nodes, instead of 24, so a long hall gets a cell about half the size. A first try with a 32,768 node budget made a closed 68 m hall take 40 s, past the 20 s bake limit, and the scene was drawn with no grid at all; the budget keeps every reference hall inside the limit. In a grid scene the fog's bounce is now the grid's mean light and the fog's ambient counts the sky only.
