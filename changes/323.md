- Add `67js/addons/crowd-skinning` (issue #323): `createCrowdRig`, `bakeCrowdPalette` and `createCrowdParts` bake a character's clip into a joint-matrix texture once and draw every instance of it as one instanced batch per material, each instance sampling its own clip time, so draw calls stop growing with the number of people. Instances are culled by a bound that covers the whole clip, and `createCrowdLods` gives coarser levels skinned with the same joints. The renderer gains small hooks for it (a `crowd` part field, `crowdJoints` and `crowdWeights` geometry attributes, a `crowdEnabled` shader uniform); skinned parts without `crowd` behave as before.
