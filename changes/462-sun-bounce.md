- Light the surroundings of the sunlit patches in a sun-lit closed room (issue #462). A lazily loaded module (`withSunBounce`, loaded with the floor sky trace) finds the patches when the scene is prepared and adds up to 6 unshadowed point lights above them, with flux = sun irradiance x lit area x albedo per channel, so the floor, walls and pillars near a patch get more light than the far end. `environment.sunBounce` is a number of 0 or more (0 off, byte for byte the look before; 1 default; above 1 stronger) in recipes, `set-environment`, the MCP schema and `docs/agent-look.md`; anything else throws a TypeError. Open scenes, rooms with no sun and baked scenes do not change. Ring luminance around the patch moves toward Cycles (`dark-shaft` mean 0.77 to 0.97, `temple-hall` 0.67 to 0.72), FLIP moves by -0.0044 to +0.0022; see `docs/lighting.md`.
