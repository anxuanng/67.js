- Add the light that emissive parts send to the rest of an open scene to the preview environment (`groundBounceEnvironment`, issue #392). Their emitted power, per colour channel, over the area of the scene without its ground, times the mean albedo over one minus it, is added to the map's `bounce` colour, so windows and lamps warm the facades and streets around them with no per-frame or shader cost. A closed room, a baked scene, a scene with only mapped emission or with next to no emission is unchanged, and `environment.emissiveSpill: false` turns it off. On `megacity-dusk` (SwiftShader against a fresh Cycles render) FLIP goes from 0.1580 to 0.1364 and the facade slices of the picture from 0.75-0.77 to 0.96-1.07 of Cycles; the seven other scenes checked draw the same pixels.
