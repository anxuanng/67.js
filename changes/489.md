- Make the mesh blend pass cheaper (issue #489). The shader reads its 24 taps from a constant table instead of computing a square root, sine and cosine per tap, the ID attachment is cleared each frame so an ID lookup needs no depth fetch, and a batch whose parts share one radius and one level keeps its frustum culling. On the nature scene at 1080p on an RTX 3060 the pass costs 0.25 to 0.58 ms of GPU time over 12 rounds (median 0.31 ms), down from about 0.55 ms; scenes without `blend` draw the same pixels.
