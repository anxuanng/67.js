- Fix the sun reaching surfaces that sit behind an open single-sided surface (part of issue #206). The sun shadow pass culled back faces, so a ray that entered a single-sided mesh from behind, such as the open edge of the terrain, wrote no depth and the sun passed through the rock. The shadow depth pass now draws both faces (`applyAlphaState` in `src/renderer.js`). `mountain-valley` with the world light at 0 reads 0.0000 on the floor (0.1727 before, Cycles 0.0003) and scores 0.1792 against Cycles instead of 0.2612; every other reference scene is unchanged to four digits and a shadow map redraw costs 0.05 to 0.1 ms more. The `foliage` crown leak is not this cause and stays open. See `bench/reference/README.md`.
