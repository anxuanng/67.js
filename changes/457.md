- `loadGltf` and `parseGltf` now open a glTF whose material has maps with different `KHR_texture_transform` (issue #457), for example a tiled base color map beside an emissive map with no transform, instead of throwing. The loader keeps the transform that is cheapest to tile to and resamples the other maps into that UV space at load, with a `warnings` entry naming the material and map. A resampled map grows to at most 4 times its source pixels and never past `maxTextureImagePixels`. Maps that cannot be resampled (a ratio that is not a whole number, a clamped or mirrored sampler, a compressed texture) still fail, now with the material named. Scenes that load files without mixed transforms draw the same pixels.
